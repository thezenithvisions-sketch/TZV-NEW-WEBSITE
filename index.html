Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you may need to know

All insights into our expertise provided in easily digestible nuggets. No question too big or small, ask away!

More Questions?

What BIM services do you offer?

Architecture, Structural and MEP BIM, Scan to BIM, drafting and CAD to Revit conversion, construction and permit documentation, shop drawings, interior documentation, 3D modeling and visualization, and custom Revit families, for architects, engineers, contractors and developers.

What types of projects do you work on?

Residential, commercial and mixed-use, hospitality, healthcare, educational and industrial projects, from villas and apartment buildings to offices, schools, hotels and data centres.

What do you need from us to get started?

We can start from PDFs, DWG drawings, point cloud data, sketches or an existing Revit file. We assess what you provide and confirm the level of detail we can achieve from that input.

Which software do you work in?

Revit is our core platform, alongside AutoCAD, ArchiCAD, SketchUp, Vectorworks, Navisworks, Lumion, Enscape and ReCap. We work in Revit 2022 through 2025 and can save down to older versions where needed.

What level of detail (LOD) do you model to?

We agree the LOD at the start of every project, from LOD 100 to LOD 500, based on the project stage and the information available in your drawings or scans.

Can you work to our standards and templates?

Yes. We work to your templates, title blocks and BIM Execution Plan, and follow ISO 19650 naming and folder conventions. If you do not have a BEP, we use a standard template and share it for your review.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A single-discipline model for a medium-sized building typically takes 3 to 10 business days; multi-discipline coordinated models take longer. Every quote includes a firm schedule.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, level of detail and deliverables. Share your drawings or a short brief and we will send a free, no-obligation quote. We respond to every enquiry within 24 hours.

Where are you based?

We have offices in North Bergen, New Jersey (USA) and Indore (India), and work as an extension of AEC teams in 10+ countries.
